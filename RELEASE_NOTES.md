# v1.4.31

- The first onion cutscene now starts Shrek's music immediately and brings him in from the fog instead of popping him in. Shrek runs toward you from the front while the camera follows him, then the cutscene spins you around and shows the shaking RUN!!! warning.
- When Shrek respawns after an onion, he runs in from the fog toward his spawn point instead of fading in at close range.
- The world boundary now has animated fog curtains on all four sides. The invisible collision walls still stop you, but the edge of the swamp now looks like dense fog.
- The outer ring of 200 trees now uses the same near and far LODs as every other tree, so they no longer disappear when you get close. They are also named like normal trees instead of FarTree.
- Trees now darken with the night phases instead of staying lit after the swamp goes dark.
- Hold C to look behind. While it is held, the camera locks onto Shrek and free-look is disabled, and a direction arrow at the bottom center shows which way you are moving relative to the camera. Your body and movement direction stay the same. Mobile gets a holdable LOOK button.
- C is no longer bound to crouch. Crouch stays on Ctrl.
- Mobile anti-aliasing still cannot be turned off, and the fog still becomes fully opaque at the effective draw-distance edge.
